Everything else in the game — the car, fuel pumps, canopy, store, shelving,
coolers, products, signage, and all UI — is procedural three.js geometry and
canvas-generated textures authored in this repository. Product labels and the
station brand are fictional (see `config/brand.js`); no real trademarks are
used. Fonts are self-hosted via next/font (Big Shoulders, Overpass, Overpass
Mono — SIL OFL). Audio is synthesized at runtime with WebAudio; no recorded
samples ship.
